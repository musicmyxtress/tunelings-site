Tunelings

Breed little singing creatures by ear. Every Tuneling's call is made on your phone from its genes, and every Tuneling is drawn from them too, so no two sound or look quite alike. Breed for the calls and looks visitors ask for, collect every sound in your call book, and chase rare ones like harmony, echo and glow.

Fully playable with VoiceOver, and made for sighted players too.
